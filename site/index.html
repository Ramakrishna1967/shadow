HOW IT WORKS

Watch. Prepare. Prove.

One process on your machine turns signals into reviewed, reversible changes.

  1. STEP 01

    Predict

    Events from GitHub, Sentry, your editor and the clock are scored into one ranked next task. Low-value work is dropped with a reason you can read.

  2. STEP 02

    Prepare in a sandbox

    The change is made in its own git worktree, inside an OS-native sandbox that must pass a five-check probe first. Your working tree is never touched.

  3. STEP 03

    Prove, then wait

    Diff, tests and a scan go onto one card. After your tap, the daemon pushes once, with an idempotency key. No tap, no side effect.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

01Does my code leave my machine?

SHADOW runs on your computer. Code is sent only to the model provider you choose, with your own key. Point it at Ollama and nothing leaves the machine.

02Can SHADOW push or merge without me?

No. Every push, pull request or comment waits for your approval. It then runs once from the daemon, never from inside the sandbox. There is no force push.

03Will it touch my working tree?

No. SHADOW works in its own git worktrees on shadow/* branches. Your checkout and uncommitted changes stay as they are.

04Which models does it use?

Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI, Google or Ollama. You set a fast tier and a strong tier, and pay the provider directly for the tokens you use.

05What happens if a fix fails its tests?

The card shows the failure with the reason. Nothing is proposed as ready, and nothing is pushed. Every drop or deferral has a reason you can read.

06Which platforms are supported?

Windows 11 (WSL2 + bubblewrap or AppContainer), Linux (bubblewrap) and macOS (sandbox-exec).
