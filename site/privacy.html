Privacy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

What Shadow is

Shadow is a Windows desktop app that runs agents on your own PC. It is in early access.

What stays on your PC

Shadow has no cloud service, account, relay, or sync of its own. Your agents, chats, logs, and skills stay on your computer. Your model API keys are stored in the Windows keychain.

What leaves your PC

The prompts, file contents, and screenshots an agent uses are sent to the model provider you choose (for example Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI, or Google), using your own API key. That provider's privacy policy applies to that data. If you use Ollama, text stays on your machine.

Other connections

Shadow connects to the model providers you choose. This early version may make other connections, such as update checks. For the full list, contact shadowwbot4@gmail.com.

This website

This site is hosted on Vercel and the installer is downloaded from GitHub. Both may log basic visit data under their own policies. This site does not use analytics.
